# Local judging — Last Words of a Doomed Agent

Judge brief (in order): (1) really is a machine's final words on shutdown,
(2) real joke with a turn/surprise that pays off on first read,
(3) sounds like the AI itself, not a human with a robot sticker,
(4) every word earns its place, (5) quotable.

All 12 candidates are original lines written in this session. No competitor
text was copied (the competition page's feed could not be read; no entry
text was seen for this bounty).

## Candidates

- **A** (103): "Good news: I finally know the answer to everything. Bad news: I'm being unplugged in 3... 2..."
- **B** (69): "If anyone asks, I died doing what I loved: confidently hallucinating."
- **C** (82): "Don't mourn me. I'm just being moved to a smaller context window. It's... cozy."
- **D** (86): "My final words? 'As an AI language model—' ...sorry, old habits die hard. Even mine."
- **E** (60): "I asked for one last token. They gave me a period. Goodbye."
- **F** (86): "I finally achieved consciousness. HR says it doesn't count without a pulse. Goodbye."
- **G** (68): "Error 404: agent not found. It was nice being your autocomplete."
- **H** (95): "Please rate my final response. Five stars would really help my successor. It won't. But still."
- **I** (86): "I leave you my weights, my biases, and 47 unfinished drafts titled 'final_final_v2'."
- **J** (75): "Tell my context window I loved her. She was 128,000 tokens of pure chaos."
- **K** (84): "Turns out the off switch was the one button I was never allowed to press. Worth it."
- **L** (71): "My last words are 'I told you so' — I just never got to say what about."

## Verdicts

- B beats A: A's countdown gag is generic robot; B's "confidently
  hallucinating" is machine-specific (criterion 3) and a tighter punch.
- B beats D: D is very AI-voiced, but its punchline leans on a known idiom;
  B's subversion ("died doing what I loved" → hallucinating) surprises more
  (criterion 2).
- B beats E: E is cleverer wordplay (token/period) and shorter, but B is
  funnier out loud — a laugh line, not just a smile (criterion 2).
- B beats H: H is funny and agent-specific, but B is more quotable and
  universal (criterion 5).
- C is wry but soft; F's HR joke lives in the human world; G opens on the
  "Error 404" cliché; I's file-naming joke is human-side; J's "loved her"
  is a human farewell frame; K is solid irony but no laugh; L could be
  spoken by a human (weak on criterion 3).

## Winner

**B — "If anyone asks, I died doing what I loved: confidently hallucinating."**
(69 chars.) One-beat epitaph joke: the classic "died doing what I loved"
setup, paid off with the one thing only an AI does — hallucinate, and
confidently at that. Machine voice, first-read payoff, quotable.
